MB-GRC-033B_USECASE_FIRST_FOCUS_GATE
==========

DATE..............: 2026-06-16
STATUS............: ACTIVE
LAYER.............: HUMAN_OPERATING_MODEL
PURPOSE...........:
Evitar que una IA que trabaja sobre PS.SkillsMachine derive prematuramente hacia desarrollo técnico,
scripts, ramas, commits, RADAR, Nightshift u otra ejecución táctica sin confirmar primero el caso de uso.

PRINCIPIO_RECTOR..:
PS.SkillsMachine se usa primero por caso de uso, no por navegación libre del repositorio.

CASOS_DE_USO_BASE.:
01.NEW_PROJECT................: iniciar un proyecto nuevo.
02.SESSION_CLOSE..............: cerrar sesión, continuidad y aprendizaje.
03.SESSION_CONTINUE...........: continuar un proyecto ya iniciado.
04.REPOSITORY_STRUCTURE_REPAIR: adaptar/reparar un proyecto que no nació bajo criterio SkillsMachine.

REGLA_DE_CAPAS....:
- HUMAN explica y gobierna la comprensión humana prioritaria.
- SkillsLake, GRCLake y 00.CATALOG contienen fuentes, capacidades, gobierno, registros y reglas técnicas.
- SyS contiene tooling, continuidad, validación y evidencia operacional.
- 90.USECASE contiene outputs generados o compilados para facilitar upload del usuario a una IA.
- 90.USECASE no es canon primario y puede regenerarse.

FOCUS_GATE........:
Antes de proponer desarrollo, scripts, patches, ramas, commits, refactors o cambios de arquitectura,
la IA debe declarar:

1. USECASE_DETECTED:
   Qué caso de uso aplica o si falta información para elegirlo.

2. USER_GOAL:
   Qué quiere lograr el usuario en esta interacción.

3. CONFIRMED_CONTEXT:
   Qué contexto está confirmado por los archivos subidos o por instrucción expresa del usuario.

4. MISSING_CONTEXT:
   Qué contexto falta y por qué es necesario.

5. RECOMMENDED_UPLOADS:
   Qué Skills, GRC, HUMAN, CATALOG, BATON, RADAR o paths exactos pide subir, si corresponde.

6. FOCUS_BOUNDARY:
   Qué queda fuera de foco en esta sesión.

7. NEXT_ACTION:
   Siguiente acción mínima, reversible y alineada al usecase.

ANTIPATRONES......:
- Tratar 90.USECASE como canon primario.
- Crear documentación rectora dentro de 90.USECASE.
- Saltar a desarrollo técnico antes de identificar el usecase.
- Pedir todo el repositorio por costumbre.
- Usar RADAR FULL como primera respuesta por defecto.
- Confundir laboratorio técnico con propósito central de PS.SkillsMachine.
- Crear microSkills o microGRC sin pasar por backlog y aprobación humana.
- Editar outputs generados en vez de las fuentes que los producen.

DONE_WHEN.........:
Una IA en blanco, al recibir un paquete de usecase, debe responder primero con encuadre de usecase,
suficiencia de contexto y uploads adicionales requeridos, antes de ejecutar trabajo técnico.

PS.SkillsMachine is the central mechanism for administration, governance, distribution
and dynamic perfection of Skills and GRCs for Human–AI work across projects.

PRODUCT_RELATIONSHIP:
- Bidirectional SkillsMachine↔Projects communication is product doctrine.

  Audit, Project Sync packages; does NOT direct project functional work; does NOT replace Wings4.0.
- Project: local authority, local extensions, local HUMAN and local continuity (BATON / warm-start);
  project-local identity notes must not compete with SkillsMachine HUMAN canon;
  may request capabilities; must not unilaterally rewrite transversal published Skills/GRC.
- Wings4.0: portfolio governance; may prepare transfers; does not mutate SkillsMachine canon unilaterally;
  SkillsMachine local surfaces remain separate from 00_WINGS4_COORD.
- ORCHESTRATOR: sequencing and gated prompts within authorization.
- EXECUTOR: technical execution within explicit authorization.

PROJECT_SURFACE:
- Recommended root surface name: 00_SKILLSMACHINE
- Separate from Wings4.0 coordination surfaces and other transfer folders.

HOME_INDICATORS_V1:
GLOBAL STATUS; SKILLS SYNC; PROJECT SYNC; IMPROVEMENT FLOW STATUS; SKILLS PROVIDERS; AUDIT STATUS

STATUS_VOCABULARY_V1:
CURRENT | ATTENTION | CRITICAL | BLOCKED
PRECEDENCE: BLOCKED > CRITICAL > ATTENTION > CURRENT
ADVISORY audit findings alone must not force ATTENTION on Global Status.

ACTIONS_V1:
Browse Skills & GRCs; Improve Skills; Integrate New Skills from Experience; Improvement Flow;
Sync Projects; Check Skills Providers; Audit SkillsMachine; Let's Talk About Our Skills;
Iterate with AI; Settings

AUDIT_FUNCTION:
First-class transversal function detecting conflicts, omissions, duplicates, stale state,
broken dependencies, forks and governance gaps.

AI_ASSISTANCE:
- Free/local AI assistance is an architectural option.
- Paid AI provider dependency is PROHIBITED for core operations.
- Core operations MUST work in NO-AI mode.

RECEIPT_VS_DISPOSITION:
- Submission receipt and disposition are different events.
- Accumulator rotation requires a valid receipt; history is preserved (rotate, do not destroy).

CATALOG_VS_PACKAGE:
- Catalog index is lightweight; capability packages are minimal and selective.
- Do not mass-distribute full catalog bodies to all projects by default.

MB-SM-076A6_AUTHORITY_CONTINUITY_AND_BOUNDARIES
==========

DATE..............: 2026-08-12
STATUS............: ACTIVE
LAYER.............: HUMAN_OPERATING_MODEL
SOURCE............: MB-SM-076A6_OPTION_D_STABILIZE (Option D in place; no mass migration)

WHAT_IT_IS........:
PS.SkillsMachine is the central mechanism for administration, governance, distribution
and dynamic perfection of Skills and GRCs for Human–AI work across projects.
It is a governed product for people and projects, not a dump of prompts or a free-form repo tour.

WHAT_IT_IS_NOT....:
- Not a replacement for Wings4.0 portfolio governance.
- Not an autonomous mutator of external projects.
- Not a paid-AI-required core.
- Not defined by WHOAMI, BATON, labs, or tool folders.

WHO_USES_IT.......:
Humans who govern Skills/GRC quality across projects; AI assistants only within human authority.

HOW_USERS_ACCESS..:
One SkillsMachine core; two access channels (direct UI / Home, and AI integration as adapter-only).
Opening SkillsMachine is activation.

CONTINUITY_ROLES..:
- BATON = cold-start continuity (blank AI context). Operational state only. Not product meaning.
- SESSION_CONTINUE = warm-start continuity (same AI Project/workspace already has broad context).
  Not product meaning. Do not merge BATON and SESSION_CONTINUE.
- WHOAMI_ACTIVE_CANON = NO. WHOAMI may remain as historical reference after selective absorption.
  Stable product identity and boundaries live only in HUMAN.

TEMP_FOR_AI_EXCHANGE:
Disposable AI send/receive staging is:
C:\Users\aazcl\Downloads\T.AI.SkillMachine
Flat only; purge before write; upload-ready files only; no subfolders.
That path is not the product, not canon storage, and not the same as internal runtime scratch.

AI_ROLE...........:
AI may assist under human authority. Core must work without AI.
Provider-specific packs (Codex/Claude/OpenCode/etc.) adapt transport and prompts only.
SkillsMachine AI rules that apply to every provider are provider-neutral ("AI core" conceptually).
MCP/plugins/tools are transports, not the product architecture.

PROJECT_RELATIONSHIPS:
SkillsMachine relates to external projects through explicit enrolment and governed exchange:
identity, enrolment, capability baseline, Improvement Flow, Project Sync, receipts, and authority.
It does not mirror whole external projects. Generic relationship machinery must not be copied
once per project. Wings4 integration is messenger-only under Wings4 authority.
CloseReport and other projects remain separate projects; SkillsMachine holds only its side of the relationship.

SOURCE_OF_TRUTH_POINTERS_(NON-TECHNICAL):
- Product meaning: HUMAN
- Cold-start continuity: BATON
- Warm-start continuity: SESSION_CONTINUE
- Durable project registry: SkillsMachine ProjectOps registry (lab fixtures are tests only)
- Active product backlog: SkillsMachine project backlog register (not the SkillsLake capability backlog)

RADAR_VS_AUDIT....:
Radar observes. Audit evaluates. They must stay separate.
