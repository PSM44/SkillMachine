FIN_90.USECASE_DISTRIBUTION_CONTRACT
==========

FOCUS_GATE........:
Antes de proponer desarrollo, scripts, patches, ramas, commits, refactors o cambios de arquitectura,
la IA debe declarar:

1. USECASE_DETECTED:
   Qué caso de uso aplica o si falta información para elegirlo.

2. USER_GOAL:
   Qué quiere lograr el usuario en esta interacción.

3. CONFIRMED_CONTEXT:
   Qué contexto está confirmado por los archivos subidos o por instrucción expresa del usuario.

4. MISSING_CONTEXT:
   Qué contexto falta y por qué es necesario.

5. RECOMMENDED_UPLOADS:
   Qué Skills, GRC, HUMAN, CATALOG, BATON, RADAR o paths exactos pide subir, si corresponde.

6. FOCUS_BOUNDARY:
   Qué queda fuera de foco en esta sesión.

7. NEXT_ACTION:
   Siguiente acción mínima, reversible y alineada al usecase.

ANTIPATRONES......:
- Tratar 90.USECASE como canon primario.
- Crear documentación rectora dentro de 90.USECASE.
- Saltar a desarrollo técnico antes de identificar el usecase.
- Pedir todo el repositorio por costumbre.
- Usar RADAR FULL como primera respuesta por defecto.
- Confundir laboratorio técnico con propósito central de PS.SkillsMachine.
- Crear microSkills o microGRC sin pasar por backlog y aprobación humana.
- Editar outputs generados en vez de las fuentes que los producen.

DONE_WHEN.........:
Una IA en blanco, al recibir un paquete de usecase, debe responder primero con encuadre de usecase,
suficiencia de contexto y uploads adicionales requeridos, antes de ejecutar trabajo técnico.

WHAT_IT_IS........:
PS.SkillsMachine is the central mechanism for administration, governance, distribution
and dynamic perfection of Skills and GRCs for Human–AI work across projects.
It is a governed product for people and projects, not a dump of prompts or a free-form repo tour.

WHAT_IT_IS_NOT....:
- Not a replacement for Wings4.0 portfolio governance.
- Not an autonomous mutator of external projects.
- Not a paid-AI-required core.
- Not defined by WHOAMI, BATON, labs, or tool folders.

WHO_USES_IT.......:
Humans who govern Skills/GRC quality across projects; AI assistants only within human authority.

HOW_USERS_ACCESS..:
One SkillsMachine core; two access channels (direct UI / Home, and AI integration as adapter-only).
Opening SkillsMachine is activation.

CONTINUITY_ROLES..:
- BATON = cold-start continuity (blank AI context). Operational state only. Not product meaning.
- SESSION_CONTINUE = warm-start continuity (same AI Project/workspace already has broad context).
  Not product meaning. Do not merge BATON and SESSION_CONTINUE.
- WHOAMI_ACTIVE_CANON = NO. Standalone WhoAmI physical residuals are deleted.
  Do not recreate. Stable product identity and boundaries live only in HUMAN.

TEMP_FOR_AI_EXCHANGE:

C:\Users\aazcl\Downloads\T.AI.SkillMachine
Flat only; purge before write; upload-ready files only; no subfolders.
That path is not the product, not canon storage, and not the same as internal runtime scratch.

AI_ROLE...........:
AI may assist under human authority. Core must work without AI.
Provider-specific packs (Codex/Claude/OpenCode/etc.) adapt transport and prompts only.
SkillsMachine AI rules that apply to every provider are provider-neutral ("AI core" conceptually).
MCP/plugins/tools are transports, not the product architecture.

PROJECT_RELATIONSHIPS:
SkillsMachine relates to external projects through explicit enrolment and governed exchange:
identity, enrolment, capability baseline, Improvement Flow, Project Sync, receipts, and authority.
It does not mirror whole external projects. Generic relationship machinery must not be copied
once per project. Wings4 integration is messenger-only under Wings4 authority.
CloseReport and other projects remain separate projects; SkillsMachine holds only its side of the relationship.

SOURCE_OF_TRUTH_POINTERS_(NON-TECHNICAL):
- Product meaning: HUMAN
- Cold-start continuity: BATON
- Warm-start continuity: SESSION_CONTINUE
- Durable project registry: SkillsMachine ProjectOps registry (lab fixtures are tests only)
- Active product backlog: SkillsMachine project backlog register (not the SkillsLake capability backlog)

RADAR_VS_AUDIT....:
Radar observes. Audit evaluates. They must stay separate.

WHAT_BELONGS_IN_HUMAN:
HUMAN is the home for human/operator context and product meaning, including:
- human authority and role;
- product identity, purpose, and boundaries;
- durable collaboration and operating constraints that are product doctrine;
- enough short human-readable context that a blank human or AI can understand
  what the project is, why it exists, and how to operate without a separate
  WhoAmI file.

WHAT_DOES_NOT_BELONG_IN_A_STANDALONE_WHOAMI:
Do not create, require, or revive a standalone WhoAmI artifact as active canon,
bootstrap dependency, continuity authority, or canonical user profile.
Session operational state for AI remains BATON (cold-start) or SESSION_CONTINUE
(warm-start). Those are not product meaning and do not replace HUMAN.

NEW_AND_FUTURE_PROJECTS:
Required operable set is HUMAN + BATON + RADAR as defined by current bootstrap
policy. A standalone WhoAmI file is not required and must not be proposed as a
mandatory initial artifact.

OPERATOR_PAYLOAD_SEPARATION:
When an operator has a durable working-profile payload (language, personal
collaboration preferences, operator-specific constraints), continuation packages
must preserve that payload if it exists for that operator.
That payload is not SkillsMachine product doctrine, not a Skill, not a GRC,
and not a default for other users or projects.
Do not copy one operator's personal requirements into HUMAN as universal defaults.

ABSORBED_FROM_WHOAMI_POLICY:
The still-useful job formerly described by 23.SKILL.WHOAMI_POLICY — a short,
understandable human snapshot of purpose and operating context — is absorbed
here. That Skill is not active product and must not be loaded, required, or
recreated. Stale project-state dumps, WhoAmI freshness gates, and WhoAmI
file-update mechanics are not promoted into HUMAN.

SHORT_HUMAN_CONTEXT_CHECKLIST:
When a human or AI needs a compact current-context snapshot for reliable work,
the minimum fields are:

- PURPOSE / PURPOSE_POINTER
- CURRENT_STATE / STATE_POINTER
- CURRENT_DELIVERABLES
- OPEN_ITEMS
- NEXT_ACTION
- CURRENT_AS_OF / DATE

HUMAN owns the durable meaning of those fields (what they are, why they matter,
and that a standalone WhoAmI file is not required to supply them).
Live operational values belong in BATON (cold-start) or SESSION_CONTINUE
(warm-start), preferably by pointer (STATE_POINTER), not by copying a second
live project dump into HUMAN.
Do not turn HUMAN into a second BATON.
Do not hard-code one operator's personal working-profile as a generic default
for other users or projects.
