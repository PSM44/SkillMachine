==========
GRC.COMPACT_UPLOAD_PACK_ONLY
==========

GRC_ID: GRC.COMPACT_UPLOAD_PACK_ONLY
SOURCE_MB: MB-SM-055J_CORE_HARDENING_COMPACT_UPLOAD_AND_ACCUMULATED_REGISTERS
REFINED_MB: MB-SM-077D
MARKER: SM-055J-GRC-COMPACT-UPLOAD-PACK-ONLY
STATUS: ACTIVE_CANDIDATE_CORE
UPDATED_AT: 2026-08-16
ABSORBS: DETU upload-pack candidate; GPT.Upload.Lock as MODE only (no standalone Skill or GRC)

CONTROL:
When a runner uses the current external AI-exchange Temp
(C:\Users\aazcl\Downloads\T.AI.SkillMachine; legacy name Temp.SkillMachine is superseded for AI exchange)
or equivalent Temp upload staging, it MUST leave a compact upload pack only.

Upload-ready Temp is disposable evidence staging. It is NEVER durable baseline
and NEVER product canon. Compact packaging does not change human or canonical
authority and does not authorize deletion of required durable evidence.

OUTPUT MODES:
- NORMAL_OUTPUT_MODE = default when file uploads are available. Produce a
  compact upload pack and ask the human to upload only that pack
  (SkillsMachine method name: DETU).
- GPT_UPLOAD_LOCK_MODE = temporary fallback when uploads are unavailable,
  exhausted, unreliable, or explicitly activated by the human.
  MODE_NAME=GPT.Upload.Lock
  This is a mode of this GRC, not a new control surface.

MANDATORY RULES:
1. TEMP_OUTPUT_POLICY must be stated as COMPACT_UPLOAD_PACK_ONLY in AI_TAIL.
2. Temp must be cleaned at script start (purge before write).
3. Raw logs, stale packages, empty evidence files, and disposable
   intermediates must not remain in Temp at the end.
4. Bounded output discipline:
   - FILES_COUNT is an upper bound of 10, not a target.
   - Use the minimum necessary artifact count.
   - Prefer one consolidated AI-readable artifact when feasible.
   - Multiple files only when technically justified.
   - Upload-ready names may be any self-explanatory files.
   - Do not require the prefix UPLOAD_*.txt.
5. Emit a package inventory listing only the files that must be uploaded.
   The inventory may live inside the single consolidated artifact.
   Do not ask the human to upload files outside that inventory.
6. If consolidation cannot keep FILES_COUNT <= 10, FAIL with
   BLOCKER=UPLOAD_PACK_OVER_LIMIT.
7. AI_TAIL must be persisted and printed.
8. Tool stdout/stderr must be consolidated into the upload-ready artifact(s).
9. An executing .ps1 may remain in AI-exchange Temp only if it is listed in
   the upload inventory. Otherwise do not leave the runner in that Temp.
10. No generated-output manual patching may be hidden inside upload staging.
11. AI-exchange Temp root must remain flat; subdirectories are forbidden.
12. Runners must disclose DB_MODIFIED, CANON_MODIFIED, COMMIT_PUSH_PERFORMED
    and BUILD_EXECUTED.

GPT_UPLOAD_LOCK_MODE:
ACTIVATE when uploads are unavailable, exhausted, unreliable, or the human
explicitly activates the mode.
DEACTIVATE when uploads are restored unless the human explicitly extends
GPT.Upload.Lock.
TOVS_ONLY_MODE=ON
MAX_PASTE_CHARS=9000
Runners must emit TOVS_SUMMARY_START / TOVS_SUMMARY_END with:
AI_TAIL_SCHEMA, MB_ID, FINAL_STATUS, BLOCKER, PROJECT_ROOT, TEMP_PATH,
TOVS_ONLY_MODE, MAX_PASTE_CHARS, DB_MODIFIED, CANON_MODIFIED,
COMMIT_PUSH_PERFORMED, BUILD_EXECUTED, WARNINGS, KEY_FINDINGS,
DECISIONS_NEEDED, NEXT_ACTION.
Do not request uploads, the full repository, or complete files by default.
Generated files may remain local; missing evidence is one pasteable block
of 9000 characters or less.
Skill/GRC opportunities detected in this mode remain candidates.
This mode does not change canonical authority and does not authorize
deletion of required durable evidence.

BLOCKERS:
- More than 10 upload-ready files requested or left for upload.
- Raw POSTPUSH/PREPUSH/RADAR/READINESS/VALIDATE logs left loose in Temp.
- Empty evidence files in the upload pack.
- Missing AI_TAIL.
- Missing TEMP_CLEANED_AT_START=YES.
- Missing or false TEMP_OUTPUT_POLICY.
- GPT_UPLOAD_LOCK_MODE without a TOVS block.
- Asking for files outside the inventory.

EXCEPTION:
A runner may leave an explicitly declared exception file only if the AI_TAIL
declares why it is needed and the file is listed in the inventory.
