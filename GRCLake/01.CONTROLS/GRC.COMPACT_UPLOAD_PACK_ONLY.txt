==========
GRC.COMPACT_UPLOAD_PACK_ONLY
==========

GRC_ID: GRC.COMPACT_UPLOAD_PACK_ONLY
SOURCE_MB: MB-SM-055J_CORE_HARDENING_COMPACT_UPLOAD_AND_ACCUMULATED_REGISTERS
MARKER: SM-055J-GRC-COMPACT-UPLOAD-PACK-ONLY
STATUS: ACTIVE_CANDIDATE_CORE
UPDATED_AT: 2026-07-05 01:04:17 -04:00

CONTROL:
When a runner uses Temp.SkillMachine or equivalent Temp upload staging, it MUST leave a compact upload pack only.

MANDATORY RULES:
1. TEMP_OUTPUT_POLICY must be stated as COMPACT_UPLOAD_PACK_ONLY in AI_TAIL.
2. Temp must be cleaned at script start.
3. Raw logs must not remain in Temp at the end.
4. The upload evidence set must not exceed 10 UPLOAD_*.txt files.
5. AI_TAIL must be persisted and printed.
6. Tool stdout/stderr must be consolidated into UPLOAD files.
7. The executing .ps1 may remain when it is stored in Temp.
8. No generated-output manual patching may be hidden inside upload staging.

BLOCKERS:
- More than 10 UPLOAD_*.txt files.
- Raw POSTPUSH/PREPUSH/RADAR/READINESS/VALIDATE logs left loose in Temp.
- Missing AI_TAIL.
- Missing TEMP_CLEANED_AT_START=YES.
- Missing or false TEMP_OUTPUT_POLICY.

EXCEPTION:
A runner may leave an explicitly declared exception file only if the AI_TAIL declares why it is needed.
