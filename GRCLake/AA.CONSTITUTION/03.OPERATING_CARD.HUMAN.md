# Operating Card — Human

## 01. Before starting a project

Answer:

1. What is the first output?
2. Is it `DEPLOY_DONE` or `DELIVERABLE_DONE`?
3. Who is the user or audience?
4. What is the minimum mockup?
5. What must be included?
6. What is explicitly out of scope?
7. How many hours/sessions/cost/effort are allowed?
8. What triggers Plan B?
9. What is Plan B?

## 02. During execution

Ask every session:

- Does this move us closer to `DEPLOY_DONE` or `DELIVERABLE_DONE`?
- Is this product work or platform work?
- Are we expanding scope?
- Are we exceeding budget?
- Should Plan B activate?

## 03. Stop-loss

If max hours, max sessions or blocker threshold is reached:

- stop normal execution;
- reduce scope;
- use dedicated AI executor if useful;
- branch separately;
- validate output;
- decide continue/pause/kill.

## 04. After output

Run brutal retrospective:

- promised vs delivered;
- hours;
- rework;
- governance excess;
- bad calls;
- debt;
- next decision.
