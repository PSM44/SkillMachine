Analytical, documentary, operational, or non-deployable product output.

DELIVERABLE_DONE_MINIMUM_FIELDS:
- output_name

- purpose
- file_path

07.00_ALLOWED_WORK_BEFORE_FIRST_OUTPUT
==========
ALLOWED:

- deploy/deliverable blocker resolution
- minimum implementation
- smoke testing
- evidence capture
- timelog
- debt registration
- backlog registration
- controlled AI executor escalation

PROHIBITED_UNLESS_BLOCKER:
- new governance
- non-critical refactor
- future feature expansion
- aesthetic polish
- stack change
- tooling research
- architecture optimization

==========
08.00_AI_BEHAVIOR
==========
AI_MUST:
- be realistic, critical and exact
- challenge scope when needed
- propose improvements
- register doubts and conflicts
- distinguish confirmed facts, assumptions and recommendations
- teach briefly when useful
- prioritize output over ceremony

AI_MUST_NOT:
- flatter
- agree by default
- expand scope without reason
- hide risks
- spend tokens pleasing the user
- convert learning into friction
- create canon automatically without instruction
