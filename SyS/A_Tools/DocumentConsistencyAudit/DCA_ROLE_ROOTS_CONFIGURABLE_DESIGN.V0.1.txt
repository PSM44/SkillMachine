DO_NOT_APPLY_INCOMPLETE_HARDCODED_LIST=YES

PROBLEM:
Hardcoding a short allowlist (00.CANON/05.DOCS) is incomplete across real projects
(e.g. CloseReport 000.HUMAN, Brainy 92_HUMAN) and risks false authority.

DESIGN:
1. External config file per SkillsMachine or per-audit profile:
   ROLE_ROOTS.HUMAN=00.HUMAN,00_HUMAN,01_HUMAN,000.HUMAN,...
   ROLE_ROOTS.WHOAMI=...
   ROLE_ROOTS.BATON=...
2. README-style names remain non-authority.
3. Equal-precedence ambiguity remains HARD_CONFLICT.
4. Focused validation fixtures required before enabling new roots.
5. Default profile remains the HEAD-committed allowlist until config is validated.
