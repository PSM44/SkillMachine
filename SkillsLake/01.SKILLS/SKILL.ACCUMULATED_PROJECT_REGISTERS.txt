==========
SKILL.ACCUMULATED_PROJECT_REGISTERS
==========

SKILL_ID: SKILL.ACCUMULATED_PROJECT_REGISTERS
SOURCE_MB: MB-SM-055J_CORE_HARDENING_COMPACT_UPLOAD_AND_ACCUMULATED_REGISTERS
MARKER: SM-055J-ACCUMULATED-PROJECT-REGISTERS
STATUS: ACTIVE_CANDIDATE_CORE
UPDATED_AT: 2026-07-05 01:04:17 -04:00

PURPOSE:
Maintain project-level accumulated registers so future IA instances can reconstruct decisions, unresolved questions, ideas, and Skill/GRC opportunities without relying on chat memory.

REGISTER SET:
1. QUESTIONS_ANSWERS.ACCUMULATED.<ProjectName>.txt
   Captures open questions, answered questions, decision-producing Q&A, and questions to revisit.

2. IDEAS.RELATED.ACCUMULATED.<ProjectName>.txt
   Captures project-related ideas, accepted/rejected/deferred ideas, and ideas converted to backlog.

3. IDEAS.UNRELATED.ACCUMULATED.<ProjectName>.txt
   Captures unrelated ideas, future projects, parking-lot items, and organizational/personal ideas not in current scope.

4. SKILL_GRC.OPPORTUNITIES.ACCUMULATED.<ProjectName>.txt
   Captures Skill candidates, Skill improvements, GRC candidates, GRC improvements, duplicates, rejected proposals, and deferred hardening opportunities.

CLASSIFICATION RULE:
- Skill: a method, procedure, capability or repeatable way to perform work.
- GRC: a control, prohibition, gate, policy, allowed/forbidden condition, or quality rule.
- Backlog: useful work not yet validated or not yet approved for implementation.
- Accumulated register: historical project memory that may later be promoted into Skill/GRC/backlog.

MINIMUM PRACTICE:
- Keep the registers flat and IA-readable.
- Include date/time, source event, status, decision, and next action.
- Do not treat a register entry as canon until promoted through review.
