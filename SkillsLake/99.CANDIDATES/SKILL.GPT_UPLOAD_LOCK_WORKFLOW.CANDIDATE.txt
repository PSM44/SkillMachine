MODE_NAME: GPT.Upload.Lock
PRIMARY_METHOD_WHEN_UPLOADS_AVAILABLE: DETU
DETU_MEANING: Download executable, execute locally, Upload maximum 10 consolidated files from Temp.
MODE_SCOPE: TEMPORARY_FALLBACK_WHEN_UPLOADS_ARE_UNAVAILABLE_OR_LIMIT_EXHAUSTED

PURPOSE:
Define a temporary no-upload / low-upload operating method for SkillsMachine and adjacent projects when file uploads are unavailable, exhausted, unreliable, blocked, or operationally undesirable.

IMPORTANT MODE BOUNDARY:
GPT.Upload.Lock is not the default permanent methodology.
When file upload is available again, the workflow must return to DETU:
- Download executable.
- Save executable in Temp path.
- Execute locally.
- Generate consolidated outputs in Temp.
- Upload maximum 10 consolidated files.
- Use those files for normal IA continuity and audit.

WHEN TO ACTIVATE GPT.Upload.Lock:
- The human cannot upload files.
- The upload limit is exhausted.
- Attachments are unreliable or temporarily unavailable.
- The human explicitly activates GPT.Upload.Lock.

WHEN TO DEACTIVATE GPT.Upload.Lock:
- Upload is available again.
- The human explicitly returns to DETU.
- The workflow requires normal artifact review and upload is possible.

OPERATING CONTEXT UNDER GPT.Upload.Lock:
- Human can paste text blocks only.
- Maximum paste budget: 9000 characters per response.
- Assistant must not depend on attachments by default.
- Scripts and runners must emit a compact pasteable final block.

REQUIRED FINAL BLOCK:
TOVS_SUMMARY_START
...
TOVS_SUMMARY_END

MINIMUM REQUIRED FIELDS:
- AI_TAIL_SCHEMA
- MB_ID
- FINAL_STATUS
- BLOCKER
- PROJECT_ROOT
- TEMP_PATH
- TOVS_ONLY_MODE=ON
- MAX_PASTE_CHARS=9000
- DB_MODIFIED
- CANON_MODIFIED
- COMMIT_PUSH_PERFORMED
- BUILD_EXECUTED
- WARNINGS
- KEY_FINDINGS
- DECISIONS_NEEDED
- NEXT_ACTION

RULES:
1. Do not request uploads by default while GPT.Upload.Lock is active.
2. Do not request the full repository.
3. Do not request complete files unless explicitly authorized.
4. Every runner must print a final pasteable TOVS block <=9000 chars.
5. Generated files may remain local on the human computer; do not require upload.
6. If evidence is missing, request a specific pasted block <=9000 chars.
7. Tables must be summarized to max 20 critical rows/columns.
8. Errors must still emit FINAL_STATUS=FAIL and BLOCKER=<exact cause>.
9. Use PASS_WITH_REVIEW only for intermediate deliverables.
10. Before asking, inspect available context, accumulated registers, Q&A, decisions and previous AI_TAIL/TOVS.
11. Runners must not clean Temp if they depend on previous outputs unless explicitly authorized or they preserve needed outputs first.
12. Runners must state whether DB, canon, Git, build or agents were modified.
13. Detected opportunities for Skills/GRC under this mode are candidates, not automatic canon.

ACCEPTANCE TESTS:
- Runner completes and prints TOVS_SUMMARY_START/END.
- Summary is <=9000 characters.
- Summary includes required fields.
- The assistant can decide next action from summary alone.
- No upload is required to continue.
- When upload is restored, next runner or response explicitly returns to DETU.
