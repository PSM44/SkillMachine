PURPOSE:
Define the mandatory DETU upload-package rule for SkillsMachine and adjacent workflows.

DETU DEFINITION:
Download executable -> execute locally -> upload a consolidated evidence package from Temp.

NON-NEGOTIABLE RULE:
The assistant must consolidate all files it considers necessary into a maximum of 10 upload files.

CLARIFICATION:
The target is not exactly 10 files. The package may contain 1 to 10 files.
If evidence, logs, CSVs, manifests, diffs, stdout, stderr, and reports would exceed 10 files, the runner must merge/fuse them into fewer consolidated files before asking the human to upload.

REQUIRED RUNNER BEHAVIOR:
1. Produce a package under Temp.
2. Emit a manifest listing only the files that must be uploaded.
3. Keep UPLOAD_FILES_COUNT <= 10.
4. Do not ask the human to upload files outside the manifest.
5. If more than 10 files are needed, consolidate first.
6. If consolidation is impossible, fail with BLOCKER=UPLOAD_PACK_OVER_LIMIT.
7. The manifest must state MAX_UPLOAD_FILES=10 and FILES_COUNT=<n>.
8. Raw stdout/stderr files should be merged into a validation/evidence bundle unless they are among the listed upload files.
9. Package files should be self-explanatory for the IA.
10. Temp can contain extra local files, but only manifest-listed files are upload-required.

ACCEPTANCE TESTS:
- FILES_COUNT is between 1 and 10.
- Manifest lists every required upload file.
- No additional upload request is needed to continue.
- The assistant can review the package from the uploaded files alone.
- The runner states DB_MODIFIED, CANON_MODIFIED, COMMIT_PUSH_PERFORMED and BUILD_EXECUTED.

FAILURE MODES:
- More than 10 files requested for upload.
- Extra logs requested after manifest upload when they could have been merged.
- Asking for exactly 10 files instead of up to 10.
- Asking for full repo or unconsolidated raw folders.

TEMP CLEANUP AND UPLOAD-ONLY RULE
Marker: MB-SM-057D-R3_TEMP_CLEAN_UPLOAD_ONLY_RULE

Before generating or adding files under Temp, the runner must delete existing Temp contents.

The final Temp content must be upload-ready:
- only files intended for upload;
- no stale package folders;
- no extra stdout/stderr fragments unless they are explicitly part of a consolidated upload file;
- no intermediate files;
- no previous-run artifacts.

If more than 10 files would be needed, consolidate/merge first.
If Temp was not cleaned before use, that is an operational non-compliance and must be corrected in the next iteration.

## DETU flat AI-exchange temp rule

Marker: MB-SM-057E-R1_TEMP_FLAT_AI_EXCHANGE_RULE
Supersedes: MB-SM-057E-R1_TEMP_SINGLE_UPLOAD_SUBFOLDER_RULE for SkillsMachine AI exchange
